# Changelog

## 0.2.0

Idle Bluetooth reads are every 8 seconds. A move still checks every second, and the menu updates when the move finishes. Long device names stay on one line.

## 0.1.17

The menu says where a device is connected, and **Move here** is the action. A move stays on **Moving…** until the other Mac has the device.

## 0.1.16

The menu reads like a Mac control menu. The other Mac’s name is a label. Each device has an icon.

## 0.1.15

**Shared** is one row per device. **Remove** is in that row’s menu.

## 0.1.14

Sharing, connecting, and removing happen in the menu. The menu bar is the icon.

## Earlier

0.1.0 through 0.1.13 taught the two Macs to allow each other, share a device, and move it without leaving it disconnected.
